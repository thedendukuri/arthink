🇮🇳 Introducing **prvaaha.** — India's financial markets, the way they deserve to be seen.

I've been building something quietly, and today I want to share it.

**prvaaha.** is a real-time financial markets platform built specifically for the Indian ecosystem — Bhārat's investors, analysts, traders, and anyone who believes the story of Indian markets is one of the most important economic stories of our generation.

Here's what it does right now:

📊 **Live indices** — Nifty 50, Sensex, Bank Nifty, Nifty IT, Auto, Pharma — with real-time prices and interactive charts. Not delayed. Not static screenshots. Actually live.

🏢 **Every listed company** — 680+ NSE-listed companies with live prices, sector breakdown, market cap, and full individual stock pages with historical charts and financial data. The goal is full coverage of all 5,500+ publicly traded companies in Bhārat.

🌍 **Global context** — Markets don't exist in isolation. The platform tracks indices across the US, Europe, Japan, China, Asia-Pacific, and Latin America on a single interactive world map, so you can see how India fits into the global picture at a glance.

📰 **Financial news, curated** — aggregated from Economic Times, Business Standard, Mint, Moneycontrol, Reuters, BBC, and more — filtered and tagged by region and category, with no noise.

---

But here's what I'm really building toward:

India is on the verge of becoming one of the top three economies in the world. Its capital markets are maturing at a pace that few predicted. And yet, the tools available to Indian investors — especially retail participants — are a generation behind what's available in the West.

**prvaaha.** is my attempt to close that gap.

The vision is a world-class financial intelligence platform, built from first principles for the Indian context. Not a Bloomberg clone. Not another screener with a dark theme. Something that takes Indian investors seriously — with the depth, design quality, and data integrity that the Indian financial ecosystem deserves.

Upcoming on the roadmap:
- Complete NSE + BSE company coverage (all 5,500+)
- Portfolio tracking and watchlists
- IPO calendar with real-time GMP data
- Macroeconomic dashboard — GDP, CPI, RBI rate cycle, credit data
- Deeper sector analysis and peer comparison tools
- Mobile-first experience
